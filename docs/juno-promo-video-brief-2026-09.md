# JUNO — Promo video brief

Date: 2026-09-02  
Goal: create a short promo video using real screen recording of the app/PWA to show the product clearly and drive installs/signups.

## Core positioning

JUNO is not a swipe-first dating app. It is an astrology and synastry app that helps people understand themselves, read relationship dynamics, and start better conversations.

The promise:

> Meet with less randomness. Start with more meaning.

JUNO should feel calm, premium, reflective, and practical. The app is not “fortune telling” and should not promise that astrology predicts love. The tone is: astrology as a conversation language, not a verdict.

## Primary message

Most dating apps make people decide too fast from photos. JUNO slows the moment down and gives users more context: natal chart, synastry, houses, planetary placements, reflections, and conversation guidance.

Short version:

> JUNO helps you understand the energy between two people before the conversation goes nowhere.

## Target audience

- People interested in astrology, compatibility, emotional patterns, and self-understanding.
- People tired of shallow dating apps.
- People who want better first messages and more meaningful connections.
- Astrology-curious users who may not know their full chart yet.

## What to show in the screen recording

Use real capture from the app/PWA. Avoid showing personal email addresses, private data, or real user conversations unless they are demo accounts.

Recommended sequence:

1. Opening / onboarding
   - Show the birth chart setup.
   - Emphasize that time and city improve accuracy.
   - If possible, show the chart reveal moment.

2. Discover / profile context
   - Show a profile with Sun, Moon, Rising when available.
   - Show that the app gives context beyond photos.

3. Synastry
   - Show the synastry score or reflection.
   - Show aspect-based insights if available.
   - Show the idea of “how your charts meet.”

4. Natal chart
   - Show planetary placements.
   - Show angles / houses if available.
   - Show that the chart is detailed but not overwhelming.

5. Conversation Guide
   - Show choosing a sign and a situation.
   - Show a practical line or advice.
   - This is one of the strongest conversion features because it turns astrology into action.

6. Premium / Cosmic Hub
   - Show premium features briefly.
   - Do not make the video feel like an ad for a paywall. The value should come first.

## Music and visual direction

Style:

- Soft cosmic / ambient / dreamy.
- Premium but not too slow.
- Avoid loud “mystic” clichés.

Visual rhythm:

- First 3 seconds need movement.
- Cut between screens every 2–4 seconds.
- Use zoom-ins on the most meaningful UI details.
- Keep text overlays minimal.

Color mood:

- Deep navy / black background.
- Rose accent.
- Gold for celestial/premium moments.
- Purple for Cosmic moments.

## Production checklist

Before recording:

- Use a clean demo account.
- Hide personal data.
- Confirm no missing translation keys appear.
- Confirm no RevenueCat debug text appears.
- Confirm prices are visible if showing the paywall.
- Confirm Conversation Guide works on web/PWA and mobile.
- Confirm chart data shown is honest: no fake Rising, no fake houses, no fake locations.

Recording:

- Record vertical 9:16 for TikTok/Reels.
- Record at least 1080 × 1920.
- If using App Store assets, also export stills in required store dimensions separately.
- Capture 2–3 takes of each feature.

Editing:

- Add captions.
- Add subtle zooms.
- Keep final video between 15 and 30 seconds for ads.
- Export as MP4.

## Compliance notes

Avoid claims like:

- “Find your soulmate”
- “Predict your perfect match”
- “Guaranteed compatibility”
- “This sign is toxic”
- “This placement means they will love you”

Use safer language:

- “may”
- “can”
- “often”
- “a way to reflect”
- “a starting point for conversation”
- “not a prediction”
